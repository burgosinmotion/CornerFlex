BBOX_CONVENTION
The analyzer stores (left,top,right,bottom) as inclusive coordinates of the
last visible pixel, not an exclusive post-bound. The continuous AABB is kept
separately and is not compared directly with the inclusive alpha bbox.

inclusive bboxes differ from continuous bounds only by normal edge quantization.
